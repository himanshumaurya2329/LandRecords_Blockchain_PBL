1️⃣ User Dashboard: Integrated In-Form Joint Registration & Actions

Addressed requirement: "How do we fill the joint registration form without leaving the page, and where does the Application ID come from?"

In-Form Co-Owners & Share Allocation Card

When toggling to Joint Ownership, the main form remains active and dynamically displays the Co-Owners & Share Allocation section between Personal Details and Land Details. Co-Owner 1 is automatically prefilled with the applicant's credentials, with customizable co-owner rows (Name, Aadhaar, Share %), up to 5 co-owners, and a live progress bar validating a 100% total.

One-Click Copy ID & Direct Action Buttons

In the Track Status table, each application now features a 📋 Copy button beside the Receipt Number (Application ID). Each row also has a direct 📊 Shares button linking to /ownership-dashboard?appId=APP-... and a 🤝 Consent portal shortcut for pending joint applications.

Automatic URL Query Parameter Hydration

Both /ownership-dashboard and /consent-panel now auto-hydrate from ?appId=... in the URL, automatically fetching and rendering the share breakdown and dispute status without requiring manual typing.

2️⃣ Official Dashboard: Dispute Freeze Guard

Addressed requirement: "When an active dispute is raised by any co-owner, officials must not be allowed to forward or approve the application."

  • Red Glowing Alert Banner: When application.isDisputed is true, a prominent red warning banner displays the reason and identity of who raised the dispute.
  • Action Button Lock: The "Forward" / "Approve & Forward" button is permanently disabled with a lock icon (🔒) and explicit tooltip: "Forwarding locked: active ownership dispute".
  • Collector Resolution Requirement: Clearly informs the Clerk, Surveyor, and Revenue Officer that this land title is legally frozen until the District Collector resolves the case.

3️⃣ Admin Dashboard: District Collector Dispute Resolution

Addressed doubt: "When a co-owner claims 50% instead of 30%, is the form updated or rejected?"

Action A: Rectify Shares (In-Place Update)

The District Collector can select Rectify Shares, enter the official Court/Collector Order Number, and adjust the exact percentage for each co-owner. The UI enforces that all shares sum to exactly 100%. Upon submitting, it calls PUT /api/land/:id/dispute/resolve with action: 'rectify_shares', which updates the ledger in-place and clears the dispute flag.

Action B: Reject Dispute

If the dispute is frivolous or lacks legal merit, the Collector can reject it with an official judicial order. The existing ownership shares remain unchanged, the dispute is dismissed, and the application is unfrozen.

4️⃣ Ownership Dashboard: Share Transfer Post-Registration

Addressed doubt: "When a co-owner sells their share, how does the transfer work and what verification is performed?"

Full Modal Dialog with Safety Validations

  • Transfer Eligibility Guard: The Transfer button is enabled only when registration status is completed and there is no active dispute.
  • From Owner Selection & Cap: The seller selects their Owner ID; the slider dynamic maximum automatically caps at their actual owned share percentage.
  • Buyer Identification: Inputs for Buyer User ID, Full Legal Name, and 12-digit Aadhaar Number.
  • Mandatory NOC Verification Checkbox: Prevents unauthorized transfers by requiring legal confirmation that a signed No Objection Certificate (NOC) has been verified.
  • Fabric Chaincode Invocation: Submits to POST /api/land/:id/transfer-share and automatically re-fetches updated ownership distribution upon success.
